Interference

The whole site is one sheet ruled with two identical gratings, one on top of the other. Where nothing has changed they line up and the page is plain. Where something has entered the sheet and pulled it into a new shape, the lower grating slips and a colour shows through as fringes.

Concept

The company's subject is a set of continuous deformations: agents enter an organisation, the organisation reorganises around them (it is not replaced), and the change spreads outward through the economy. We express this with an optical fact, not a diagram. When two superimposed gratings differ by a displacement field, the moiré fringes are the contour lines of that displacement. A reorganised region carries its history as fringes. A region that has fully shifted looks uniform again, only in a new colour. At no point do we draw a firm, a box or a label.

The same rule shapes the navigation. The five pages (Home, Essays, About, Team, Contact) are five regions of the sheet, always in the same order, always on screen. Opening one stretches it; the others fold to narrow strips at its sides, and the fold shows in the strips as strain. Moving between pages is a continuous deformation of one surface: nothing is cut, nothing leaves, and the topology never changes.

Structure

  • Home. A short, sticky scene (about 2.2 viewport heights) in four continuous states. Rest: plain lilac, "Corollary Labs" latent in the gratings, and the cursor bends the sheet like a lens. Entry: five points glide in from the edge and each one pulls the sheet into rings. Spread: from each point a front of fringes travels outward and the fronts merge. Settled: the whole sheet has shifted and turned rose, and the name inverts. Directly after that come [About — one paragraph], three stretch-links (About · Team · Contact) and the latest essays.
  • Essays / Essay. A band of sheet with a front halfway across it, then a plain index of rows. Essays are read in a calm Newsreader column with sidenotes; figures are interference plates.
  • About. The title latent in the band, one point with its rings, then [About — one paragraph], two paragraphs and a colophon.
  • Team. Four small displacements in the band. Each person is a disc of two ring gratings; hovering moves one of them.
  • Contact. The sheet at rest. The email is set very large and stretches on hover.

Interaction model

  • Folds replace the navbar and the footer-only rule. Every other page is always one click away and always visible as a strip. This breaks Plotter's "no nav" default on purpose: here the nav is the idea (reorganisation without removal).
  • Page transitions squash the open page horizontally, without reflow, into its strip while the new page stretches out of its own. A wave of strain crosses the sheet from one to the other (about 1.1 s).
  • Hovering a fold relaxes its strain and stretches its label from condensed to expanded (the Anybody width axis).
  • Cursor: a moving lens of fringes. Click on the sheet: a ring travels out and decays, a small diffusion you can set off yourself.
  • Phones: the folds become a strip across the top: five segments, with the open one wider.
  • Reduced motion: no sticky scene; a single still frame of the spread state, instant page changes. Without WebGL, CSS gratings produce real moiré and the titles show as text.

Logo options

A · Latent disc. A square of ruled lines; inside a circle the lower grating is shifted half a pitch, so the disc appears only as colour. This is the site's mechanism in one mark, and it is the favicon.

B · Two centres. Two identical sets of rings, one displaced. Their interference draws the mark, which changes whenever the displacement does. It animates on hover.

C · Folding wordmark. "Corollary Labs" set in one continuous width gradient, from 150 % down to 50 %: the name as a region being folded.
