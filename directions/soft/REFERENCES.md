# Soft — references

Captures are local only, in `research/soft/` (gitignored). Nothing here is copied; each reference gives one idea.

## Awwwards

1. **Lusion v3** — https://lusion.co — Awwwards Site of the Day (2 Oct 2023), and Site of the Year.
   What to take: matte, tactile objects moved by soft physics on a pale lavender ground. Objects that seem to have weight and give way to each other.
   Why: it proves that real-time rendering can feel soft and physical rather than glossy. We take the tactility and the easing, not the 3D objects.

2. **Stone and Style** (Kenta Toshikura) — https://stonestyle.co.th — Awwwards Site of the Day (16 Apr 2020).
   What to take: a single stone as the whole hero, with a great deal of air around it and very few words.
   Why: it sets the scale of restraint. One object, carefully lit, carries a page.

3. **Kenta Toshikura — Portfolio** — https://kentatoshikura.com — Awwwards Site of the Day (25 Feb 2021).
   What to take: one object seen through one aperture, with slow transitions between states. The page changes state; it does not stack sections.
   Why: it matches the founder's ask for a few continuous states rather than many screens.

4. **Web Graphics Experiments** (Keita Yamada) — https://experiments.p5aholic.me — Awwwards Honorable Mention (30 Aug 2018).
   What to take: shader-rendered relief, where type and surface share one material and one light.
   Why: our headings rest in the relief as clearings, lit by the same light as the ground.

5. **Unseen Studio** — https://unseen.co — Awwwards Site of the Day (6 Feb 2023).
   What to take: a flat pastel ground, one small centred element and a quiet entrance. It is confident about emptiness.
   Why: it sets the colour temperature. A pale, warm ground can feel designed rather than generic.

## Beyond Awwwards

6. **Karesansui (dry landscape gardens), e.g. Ryōan-ji, Kyoto** — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ry%C5%8Dan-ji
   What to take: raked lines that part around stones and close into rings, never cut.
   Why: this is the central image of the site. Lines route around what is placed among them. We make it abstract (no gravel, no temple) and literal in the maths (level sets).

7. **Iñigo Quilez, smooth minimum and distance-field articles** — https://iquilezles.org/articles/smin/
   What to take: the idea that soft union of shapes is a continuous function, so forms can merge and separate without seams.
   Why: technical basis for rendering the ground and stones analytically in one shader, so every change is a continuous deformation.
