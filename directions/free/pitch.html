Direction · free · pitch

Loops

One continuous surface of Truchet tiles, the same two quarter-circle arcs in every square. The arcs join into closed loops. An agent is one tile that carries colour. Tiles never leave the surface; they turn. Each quarter-turn rewires which loops are joined. Colour travels only along connections, so it spreads when the surface reorganises around the agent, never by adjacency alone. The whole plane can also be twisted and released (a swirl is an isotopy: every line stays unbroken).

Nothing is explained on the page. The idea is carried by one rule you can play with: hover turns a tile, click places an agent, dragging stirs the plane. The field persists across pages (Astro view transitions keep the canvas alive), so navigating re-scales and re-colours the same surface instead of loading a new picture.

Why this and not the other versions

Plotter draws a firm; Organic grows a cell; Proof writes a paper; Tableau fills a table. Loops has no diagram at all: it is a pattern with a physics. No labels, arrows or boxes. Flat saturated colour instead of paper; a graphic, poster-like surface instead of an illustration.

Key frames (drawn live below)

Structure

  1. Home: 240vh total. Sticky field story (hero → agent → wave + twist, three continuous states), then quickly: [About — one paragraph], latest three essays, large links About / Team / Contact, footer.
  2. Essays: an index of large titles; each essay carries a generated 3×3 loop emblem (seeded by its slug, longest loop in acid).
  3. Essay: the surface turns to lilac paper with violet ink for reading; the emblem heads the page; the field fades behind the text.
  4. About: huge cells (a few large loops) behind [About — statement] and [About — paragraphs].
  5. Team: each person is a loop emblem in place of a portrait, [Name] / [Role].
  6. Contact: [Email] set very large; the field reacts strongly to the cursor.
  7. Navigation: the mark + name top-left, four plain words top-right (Essays, About, Team, Contact). Breaks Plotter's footer-only rule because the field is full-bleed on every page and there is no footer in view for most of the time. The footer repeats the links. Bottom-right corner left free.
