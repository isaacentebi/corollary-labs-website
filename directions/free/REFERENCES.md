# Loops (`free`) — references

Captures are local only, in `research/free/` (gitignored).

## Awwwards

1. **Anime.js** — https://animejs.com — Awwwards Site of the Day (6 May 2025) and Site of the Month (May 2025).
   *What we take:* motion that staggers outward from a single point across a grid (its "stagger from centre" idea), and a home page that is one instrument rather than a set of sections. *Why:* the quarter-turn wave that crosses our field on scroll and on every page change is the same gesture, used to carry diffusion instead of decoration.

2. **Tolis C.** — https://tol.is — Awwwards Honorable Mention (10 July 2026). A portfolio made of generative maths and physics studies.
   *What we take:* each scene is one rule you can play with (a line field, a vortex), with almost no text around it. *Why:* our page has no diagram; it has one rule (tiles only turn, colour moves only along connections) and lets hover, click and drag reveal it.

3. **Lusion v3** — https://lusion.co — Awwwards Site of the Day (2 October 2023).
   *What we take:* tactile, playful objects on a saturated, flat colour, with confident simple type. *Why:* permission to commit to one strong non-neutral colour (a full violet ground) instead of paper or black.

4. **Gil Huybrecht** — https://gilhuybrecht.com — Awwwards Site of the Day (21 September 2026).
   *What we take:* information architecture first, a flat index with no hero ceremony, plain labels. *Why:* the founder wants the business content (About, Essays, Team, Contact) reachable in a few seconds; after the short field story, our home page is a plain index of solid blocks.

## Outside Awwwards

5. **Christopher Carlson, "Multi-Scale Truchet Patterns"**, Bridges 2018 — https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2018/bridges2018-39.html
   *What we take:* the Truchet tile (two quarter-circle arcs) as a complete visual language: every configuration makes closed curves; change the tiles' turns and the curves re-join. Tile scale changes by page (small cells for the economy view, huge cells on About).
