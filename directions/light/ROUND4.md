# Light, round 4: everything frontal

The rule for this round is that light sits on the picture plane. There is no ground plane, no perspective, no sky over earth, no drawn outlines and no rows of lamps. Each scene is a field, a band, a volume or a line of light facing the viewer, the way Pastine, Brindle, Niesche, Ando, Evertz and Vieux hang on a wall. One line runs through the whole site: the firm's boundary, a Brindle/Ando line of light with both sides glowing in related hues. It is not a horizon.

**First screen (and load): Miya Ando, *Meditation Blue Black* (2013); Turrell's Skyspace sequences.** A frontal blue-black field, with an electric blue band of light across it at about 60% height. Above the band the field glows blue; below it, a related violet-blue, so the two sides are neither sky nor ground. The page opens in this field's own dark colour, with the headline in near-white at full contrast. Only the light comes on: the band brightens and its glow spreads slowly, as the ceiling light changes in a Skyspace. Nothing pops from cream or grey.

**Inputs and outputs: Ando, *Hakanai* and *Meditation Blue Black*; Pashgian's lenses.** The same line on a deep steel-blue field. To the left of a disc of light, the line is warm amber (what comes in). To the right it is cool silver-blue (what goes out). The disc has no outline. It shows only by brightness and by refraction: inside it, the field and the line are magnified and the line sits slightly higher, as a meniscus. The only lens reference is Pashgian's, frontal.

**Automation: Pashgian's cast-resin lenses; Brindle, *Veil VI* (2024), in ember.** The field turns to a deep ember-black. Inside the disc, light fills from below the meniscus line, and the line rises as the disc fills: first to about half (execution), then almost to the top (planning). This meniscus is the main event. The edge still comes only from brightness and refraction. There is no ring or donut phase, and the lilac smear is gone.

**New combinations: Jonny Niesche's voile works (*Schein Blossom*, *Atoms Encode*); Larry Bell's coated glass; Andy Moses's iridescence.** A deep violet ground. Three soft fields of light (lilac, rose and cyan-blue), each a squircle of voile with edges several percent of the screen wide, drift from separate places into a new overlapping arrangement. They mix additively (screen), so each overlap is brighter and a new hue, capped short of white. There are no gutters, caps or swatches, and on phones the scene is full-bleed.

**Continuous deformation: Pashgian's smoky green and amber; Brindle's strata; Menchelli's lit crease.** A deep smoky-green field with the amber line across it. The line bends into an S without breaking. Then the cut becomes an event: a real dark gap opens, the light pools at the two severed ends, the ends close, and the join flashes brighter once (a weld) before settling. There are no tick marks and no ghost line.

**Diffusion (and Contact as its conclusion): Gabriele Evertz, *Grays + the Expanded Spectrum* (2014) and *Be Thou The Rainbow* (2025); Pastine's soft band edges; Ana Montiel's halos.** Eleven adjacent soft vertical fields, all dark neutral at first. Adoption is a colour change: each field turns to its own chromatic hue, rising outward from the firm's line. The turn starts at one field and passes to its neighbours with uneven delays. Some fields turn late and some only partly. Contact is the finished field, with every band turned to deep, saturated hues and the words in white over them.

**Reading surfaces (Research, essays, Company): Ando, *Hakanai* (2013).** A pale silver-blue field with a faint frontal band of light and nothing else. Dark ink.

**Seams and length.** The canvas owns the background everywhere, and the body colour follows the field, so no page shows a bare edge. Text never fades to reach contrast: its colour switches with the light behind it, and sections holding text stay at full opacity. Thinking is about seven screens: 120vh per idea, a short end, no dawn section. Home is about 3.5–4 screens.
