Direction · plotsoft · pitch

Plotter,
softened.

Same story, same figures, same pages. What changes is the drawing and the light. The rectangles, hatching and trembling ink of the plotter become rounded boundaries, curved wires and slowly breathing shapes, set in a warm mist instead of white paper. The green stays, but as a glow on the lines instead of a flat ink. Motion is eased and springy: things settle into place instead of snapping.

Per the founder's constraint, the home story is compressed to about four screen heights: the hero field, then the dive, the firm reorganising in three figures, diffusion, and the torus. Short blocks follow for About, the latest essays and links to About, Team and Contact.

What changes, and why

PaletteWhite paper and hard green become warm mist and graphite with one jade accent. The softness comes from atmosphere (two faint radial hazes and fine grain), not from pastels. Contrast stays high enough for real reading.
LineRectangles become rounded, breathing outlines (the firm's boundary is a squircle whose edge moves slowly). Straight wires become curves, and the instruction bundles hang like cords. Hatching becomes a soft tonal wash that turns pale jade as agents spread. Every line has round caps and joins. The boil filter is gone, replaced by geometry that breathes.
PointsSquare plotter dots become round, soft points. Nodes on the adoption front glow, and click shockwaves travel as soft rings. Nodes sit on springs, so the pointer and shockwaves leave a jelly-like wake instead of a rigid push.
MotionScroll is smoothed again by a critically damped spring before it drives the diagram. Agents arrive with a slight overshoot (back-out), and the mesh settles with a damped elastic. Captions cross-fade with a blur-to-sharp transition.
TorusSame homotopy: sheet → twist → tear → re-glue into a torus → it turns green. It is drawn with round points and depth-faded curves, with a glow on the green front and a soft contact shadow underneath.
TypeFraunces with its SOFT axis pinned at 100, so the terminals are rounded and the serif stays precise. It replaces Instrument Serif for display. Labels move from Space Mono to DM Mono, which is quieter and rounder. Inter Tight stays for body text.
LogoThe name as a point cloud stays the hero. For everywhere else we add a ring mark: the coda's torus reduced to two soft ellipses with one jade node on its rim, placed beside the Fraunces wordmark.
