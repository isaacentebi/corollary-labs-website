# Plotsoft — references

Plotsoft is Plotter made softer: the same story, figures and pages, drawn with rounded lines in a warmer, hazier light.
The references below are about **how things are drawn and how they move**. None of them is about layout or copy.
Local captures are in `research/plotsoft/` (gitignored, not ours to publish).

| # | Site | Awwwards | What we take | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Montfort** — mont-fort.com (Immersive Garden) | Site of the Month, Jun 2025 · awwwards.com/sites/montfort | The **atmosphere**: one hazy, low-contrast colour range, air around every object, thin labels in one tonal accent, a small hairline circle used as an interface element. | It feels soft without looking cute. The palette carries the softness and the drawing stays thin and exact. We use this for Plotsoft's warm mist ground and graphite ink. |
| 2 | **David Whyte Experience** — davidwhyte.com/experience (Immersive Garden) | Site of the Month, Dec 2024 · awwwards.com/sites/david-whyte-experience | The **line quality**: a single hairline circle that trembles and breathes as it loads, on warm grey paper, with a humanist serif. | It is a living line drawn with no ornament. Our boundaries, agents and rings breathe the same way: the geometry itself wobbles slowly, with no mechanical jitter filter. |
| 3 | **Igloo Inc** — igloo.inc (abeto) | Site of the Year 2024 · awwwards.com/sites/igloo-inc | The **rendering**: soft glowing points and fine lines floating in fog, set against a very precise, small monospace interface. | Glow and haze sit next to exact type. We borrow the glow for the field's front nodes, the agents and the green torus, and pair it with a quiet mono (DM Mono) for labels. |
| 4 | **Tracing Art** — getty.edu/tracingart (Resn for Getty) | Site of the Month, Jul 2025 · awwwards.com/sites/tracing-art | The **motion and hierarchy**: objects drift with springy, weighted easing around one large, calm serif; pill-shaped controls and soft rounded panels. | It shows that a scholarly institution can be warm and still serious. We take the springy settling of objects and the strong serif-over-mono hierarchy. |

Also looked at and not used: Illoca (Unseen Studio; a product page), Warm & Fuzzy, L.I.S.A. (Locomotive), Why Zero, Lusion, Siena Film Foundation, The Line Studio, Floema. Most were product pages or too loud. Unseen Studio and Immersive Garden's own sites had nice ideas but did not add much beyond refs 1 and 2.
